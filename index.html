For grown-ups

Each level teaches new letter sounds, then four rounds: Meet, Listen, Build, and Read aloud. In the last round your child reads words into the microphone. Works best in Chrome or Safari with the microphone allowed. If the mic can't hear them, a "Grown-up check" button lets you approve. Sound order follows the s-a-t-p-i-n phonics sequence used in many schools.
